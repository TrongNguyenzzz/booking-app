A Traveler's Rest

The road stretches far beneath the fading sun,
Through cities old and valleys yet unknown,
Each step a story waiting to be spun,
Each room a place to briefly call our own.

We book a bed beneath a foreign sky,
Where strangers smile and lanterns softly glow,
The world feels small when kindness wanders by,
And every door becomes a chance to grow.

So pack your bags and let the journey start,
With open eyes and wonder in your chest,
For every trip will leave upon your heart
The quiet joy of finding somewhere to rest.
