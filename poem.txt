The Traveler's Rest

Through winding roads and skies of gray,
The weary traveler finds their way
To doors that open, warm and bright,
A place to rest throughout the night.

The city hums beyond the glass,
As hours of wandering slowly pass.
A pillow soft, a blanket warm,
A shelter from the evening storm.

Tomorrow brings another mile,
Another city, another smile.
But here, between the dusk and dawn,
The world is still, the worry gone.

So book your room and lay your head,
Let dreams replace the miles you tread.
For every journey needs a pause,
A quiet place without a cause.
