The Code We Write

Lines of logic, row by row,
A quiet craft that few will know.
We shape the world with keystrokes light,
Turn abstract thought to working sight.

A function here, a loop right there,
Each variable placed with care.
The compiler hums, the tests go green,
The finest art you've never seen.

Through bugs we wade, through errors deep,
Through late-night watches, losing sleep.
But when it runs without a flaw,
We sit back, stunned, in silent awe.

For in these lines of black and white,
We find a strange and pure delight --
The joy of building, bit by bit,
A world that runs because we writ.
